What to know first

Upload a front-facing photo and the service creates a short story and original image around one of several historical roles.

Possible roles include a ruler, warrior, scholar, blacksmith, carpenter, farmer and more. Reward comparisons are playful estimates, not historical payroll data.

Use the result as a light story to compare with friends, not as a real claim about identity or personality.

Frequently asked questions

Can a face photo prove a real past life?

No. The result is a fictional entertainment character inspired by a photo and cultural symbols.

How is the character image made?

Your photo is used as a visual reference for a creative scene matching the selected role and period.
